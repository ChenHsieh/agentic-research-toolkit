How it works

Two ideas carry the whole site: what an agent actually is, and the one rule that keeps it useful instead of dangerous. Five minutes here saves you a lot of confusion later.

Chat versus agent

The same underlying assistant shows up in two very different shapes:

  • Plain chat can only read what you paste or attach. It never touches your computer. This is Level 1, and it is where most useful work happens.
  • An agentic tool can read and search your files, and change them if you allow it. "Agentic" just means it can take actions, not only talk. That starts at Level 2.
🛡️

The one rule. The assistant drafts and proposes. You are the record of truth, and you verify before you rely on anything. At Level 1 that is automatic (it cannot touch your files). At Levels 2 and 3 you keep it true by leaving approval on and asking the tool to quote its sources.

The two habits

The rule breaks into two habits that reappear on every page:

  • Protect your data. Know what leaves your machine before you send it. Unpublished, patient, or embargoed data needs care. See Protect your data.
  • Verify the output. Never trust a confident answer on its own. See the Spotting errors checklist.
⚠️

Verification drift. More capable models and hands-off agents feel more trustworthy, but they still invent citations and numbers with total confidence. The more power you hand over, the more you check, not less.

The capture → draft → verify loop

Everything on this site is one loop, and the last step is always yours:

  1. Capture. Get the words down however is easiest, including dictation while your hands are busy.
  2. Draft. The assistant cleans up shorthand, fixes the format, and flags what is missing, without inventing values.
  3. Verify. Both capture and draft mishear things, numbers and units most of all. You read it against what you saw, and then it is yours.

The ramp, and how to pick a level

Three levels, each usable on its own. Start at Level 1 and climb only as far as your work needs.

LevelWhat it isSetup
Level 1Plain chat cleans up notes. Nothing touches your files.None
Level 2Give an agentic tool read/write access to a copy of your files.Some
Level 3A standing setup: house rules, a one-page brief, a decision log.More, once per project

Not sure where you land? The swipe test on the home page asks two questions and recommends a level.
