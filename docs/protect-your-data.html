Protect your data

Before you paste or open anything real, know where it goes, whether it can be kept or used to train a model, and keep unpublished, IRB, or patient data out of the wrong places. This is one of the two habits that run through every page.

Match the tool to how sensitive the data is

Your data is…Do this
Made-up practice text, or already publishedRelax and just learn the tool.
Unpublished, identifiable, or embargoedTurn training off first, remove names and identifiers, and keep files local rather than piping them through a connector.
Genuinely sensitive (real patient information)Use a paid API key or an Enterprise/Team plan (not trained on by default) with a signed HIPAA agreement (a "BAA") in place, or simply de-identify first. verify which plans qualify
⚙️

Turn training off before pasting anything real. Claude: Settings > Privacy. ChatGPT: Settings > Data Controls. verify menu names

🔒

If you review manuscripts or grants, that text is confidential under the terms of peer review. Never paste it into any third-party AI tool, even a paid tier with training turned off. Work from your own notes instead. See Skills by role.

Honest holes, stated plainly

  • Turning off training has a carve-out: chats flagged for safety review can still be looked at. verify
  • "Zero data retention" is something you request and qualify for, not an automatic setting, and it does not cover ordinary consumer or Team chats. verify
  • Connectors are the quiet leak: each one sends real file or email contents out to a company's servers.
  • A HIPAA BAA covers only specific plans and endpoints, never a personal Plus or Pro plan. verify
  • You do not need a special security gateway, a VPN, or a compliance consultant to start.

Durable memory (the safe place for facts)

The other half of protecting your data is not scattering it across throwaway chats. Move any fact you must keep out of chat memory and into a durable file.

  • A fresh chat plus a short context pack at Level 1.
  • /clear in Claude Code when you start something unrelated; /compact to declutter while staying on the same task (it also compacts automatically near the limit).
  • A house-rules file plus a dated decision log when facts must survive a reset entirely.

Compaction throws away detail, so treat anything important as verify-do-not-trust. Keeping notes in a durable file also means less sensitive material floating around in throwaway chats.
