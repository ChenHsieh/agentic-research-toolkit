# An on-ramp to agentic research

A gentle, honest guide for wet-lab scientists who want to try these tools for keeping cleaner
notes and lab documentation, and who have never used an agentic (coding or "agent") tool
before.

This is the beginner front door to the [Agentic Research Toolkit](https://github.com/ChenHsieh/agentic-research-toolkit).
The rest of the toolkit (the [skills](https://github.com/ChenHsieh/agentic-research-toolkit/tree/main/skills)
and the [universal setup](https://github.com/ChenHsieh/agentic-research-toolkit/tree/main/setup))
is written for people already comfortable with these tools. This guide is for getting to that
point. No git experience assumed. No coding background assumed. Some healthy skepticism
assumed, and welcomed.

## A note on the word "agentic"

An **agentic** tool is one that can take actions for you, such as reading and searching your
files or running a check, not just chat back. Plain chat (Level 1) is where most people start,
and it is genuinely useful on its own. "Agentic" is the step up you take only when the work
calls for it. This guide is the ramp from one to the other.

## The one idea to take away first

> **The assistant drafts. You are still the record of truth.**

An assistant can turn your shorthand into a clean notebook entry, catch a missing field, or
keep a project's documentation consistent. It can also misread your shorthand or quietly
invent a detail that was never there. So the workflow is always: the assistant produces a
draft, you check it against what you actually observed at the bench, and only then does it go
into your real notebook. Nothing overwrites your records without you looking at it first.

## The two objections, answered up front

**"Will it corrupt my data?"**
Not the way this guide uses it. At Level 1 you paste text into a chat window and read the
answer. The tool never touches your files. It hands you a draft; you decide what is true and
type the final version yourself. At Levels 2 and 3, where an agentic tool can read files, the
rule is that it proposes changes and you approve them.

**"Do I need to learn to code?"**
No. Level 1 needs nothing but a chat window and copy-paste. You can stop after Level 1 and
still get real value.
