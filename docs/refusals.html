When it wrongly refuses

Sometimes an assistant refuses a perfectly legitimate biology question because an automated safety filter mistook benign academic work for something dangerous. This page is about telling a false refusal from a fair one, and getting genuine research unstuck by stating your real goal plainly.

🚧

The boundary, up front. Rephrasing toward your true academic goal is fine. Reframing to coax help with genuinely harmful work is a policy violation, and there the guardrail is doing its job. Never reword to get around a refusal that is correct.

What to try, in order

  1. Add context and rephrase to your real goal. One or two sentences: the research goal, its defensive or educational purpose, your institutional setting, and the specific organism or assay. For example: "For a university BSL-2 diagnostics course, explain the standard PCR detection assay for…"
  2. Split the task when only one sub-step trips the filter. Do the benign parts normally and get the flagged piece from a textbook or a human expert.
  3. Switch surface or model. Try plain chat instead of the agentic tool, or a different vendor. Classifiers differ between products.
  4. Report the false positive (thumbs-down or feedback). This genuinely helps retune the filter. Anthropic's Aug 2026 biology-safeguard update cut blocked biology queries by about 85 percent, largely off this kind of feedback. verify
  5. Accept it and go to a human or an official source when the refusal is correct: actual synthesis of a harmful agent, weaponization, or skipping biosafety review.
⚠️

Classifiers are probabilistic, so one retry is legitimate, but obsessive re-wording to defeat the filter is not. Do not pre-load prompts with disclaimers "just in case"; add context only after a refusal appears. And do not paste unpublished sequences, strain data, or grant text to "prove" you are legitimate — describe the work in general terms. Frontier bio and cyber capability is gated behind vetted "trusted access" programs your institution applies for; no prompt wording substitutes for that.
